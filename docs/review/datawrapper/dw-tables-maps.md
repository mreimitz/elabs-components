# Datawrapper — Tables & Maps feature catalogue (competitive gap analysis input)

Researched 2026-09-18 via WebFetch/WebSearch of datawrapper.de product pages, Academy (academy.datawrapper.de) and Blog. Everything below is from primary Datawrapper sources unless marked **[unverified]** (inferred, or the source was summarised and the exact option name could not be confirmed). Option names are quoted verbatim where the source gave them.

Editor model that applies to all of this: Datawrapper is a 4-step wizard — **1 Upload data → 2 Check & Describe → 3 Visualize (tabs: Refine / Annotate / Layout) → 4 Publish & Embed**. Almost every option named below lives in step 3. Tables and maps are "responsive by default" (iframe embed + `datawrapper.on` events).

---

## PART A — TABLES (`/tables`)

Product page positioning: "Show your data clearly with tables" · "Responsive and interactive data tables" · "Go beyond the grid by adding visualizations" · "Customize your table further". Three headline sub-types: **Data tables**, **Heatmaps**, **Tables with mini-charts**.

Feature timeline (blog): Feb 2016 first table tool · **May 15 2019** "Responsive tables with sparklines, bar charts and sticky rows" (big rewrite) · **Oct 21 2020** heatmaps for tables · **Nov 30 2022** sticky first column · **2024** heatmaps for _text_ columns (via `@@` notation) · 2023–26: no other table-specific launch posts found (the 2024 recap lists only text-column heatmaps for tables).

## PART B — MAPS (`/maps`)

Product page claims: "over 4,000 basemaps" (countries, municipalities, election districts) · "over 50 special cartograms and hexagon maps" · custom basemap upload · 3 map types: **Choropleth**, **Symbol**, **Locator**. Variants of symbol maps: **Spike maps** (Oct 2020) and **Arrow maps** (Oct 2024). Cartograms (Dorling circles; Feb 2018) and hexagon/tile basemaps are _basemaps_, not separate map types (Academy "Maps you can create": choropleth, symbol, spike, arrow, locator).

Rendering: choropleth/symbol rendered on **canvas** (not SVG) since Sep 2020, geometry pre-projected server-side; locator maps are **MapLibre/OSM vector tiles, Web Mercator**, and are _static after publishing_ (no reader zoom/pan by design; tooltips only). Timeline: locator maps Oct 2018 · custom map upload Jun 2018 · callout lines May 2019 · vignettes Feb 2020 · region area markers May 2020 · pattern fills (locator) Aug 2020 · choropleth/symbol redesign Sep 2020 · place labels May 2021 · new choropleth data upload Sep 2021 · new symbol data upload Dec 2022 · **crop to data + inset maps Apr 2024** · **pattern overlay (choropleth) Sep 2024** · **arrow maps Oct 2024** · **globe projection in locator maps Jul 2025** · **new marker editor Dec 2025** · **arrows in locator maps Jun 2026**.

## PART C — Datawrapper "River"

- `https://app.datawrapper.de/river/favorites?offset=0` → **HTTP 401** (requires login). `https://river.datawrapper.de/` → **401** as well. `https://www.datawrapper.de/river` → **404** (page does not exist). So the gallery itself could not be inspected; description below is from the blog, FAQ and Academy.
- **What it is**: launched **Jan 30 2018** as "a wire service for charts" — a public feed where any Datawrapper user can opt-in (step 4 Publish: "Add your chart to our new River") to share a chart/map/table for free reuse. Reusers click **"Reuse this chart"** → a copy opens in their account at step 3; they may change chart type, headline, description, annotations, colours and styling, but the **data and the source attribution/backlink are locked**. Submission form allows notes, byline, external link, keywords. Views are attributed to the reuser, not the creator. Custom layouts (paid plans) are preserved on reused charts.
- **Favorites** (Oct 26 2023): a hand-picked list curated by the Datawrapper team "at least weekly", judged on "content, style, or how they use Datawrapper features"; three latest favourites shown on the River home page, full list on `/river/favorites`.
- UI (per Academy "How to use the River"): "Most recent visualizations" feed, "Our River favorites", topic search; filter by chart type not documented **[unverified]**.
- Relevance for gap analysis: River is a _content-sharing/reuse_ network, not a component feature — no equivalent needed in a component library, but it is Datawrapper's de-facto examples gallery (alongside Academy "Examples of Datawrapper tables / heatmaps / choropleth / symbol / locator maps" pages, which are public).
