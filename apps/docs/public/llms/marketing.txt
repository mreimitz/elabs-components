## Components

- CTASection — Closing conversion band — one message, one action.
  - avoid: Two competing primary Buttons in the band — demote one to secondary/outline.
  - avoid: Manufactured urgency or scarcity copy — that is a dark pattern, not a conversion technique.
- FeatureGrid — Grid of capability cards below the hero.
  - avoid: Three identical icon-tile-above-heading cards — the canonical landing-page tell; vary the structure (bento, split, asymmetry).
  - avoid: Shipping placeholder feature copy as real content — a dead showcase reads as a demo.
- Hero — Above-the-fold marketing headline, subcopy and the primary call to action.
  - avoid: Filler verbs in the headline — name what the product literally does instead.
  - avoid: A hero image without explicit width/height (and fetchpriority) — it is the page's largest layout-shift risk.
  - avoid: Version/beta eyebrows in the hero — a marketing-surface tell; drop it unless the brief is about the launch.
