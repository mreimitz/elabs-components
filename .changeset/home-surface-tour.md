Surface tour for the elabs-ai.com site (`apps/home`). `@elabs-ai/components-ui` gains `SurfaceTour` (a sticky tab list over a fixed-height frame that keeps its height across tabs, crossfades content, deep-links through the URL hash and lazy-loads each tab's surface on hover, focus or click), `SurfaceTourActions` (Open in Storybook, Copy prompt and Scaffold, built on `CommandChip`) and `AffordanceHint` (a one-time, `aria-hidden` hint pill that shows once per tab per session and hides on the first interaction). All copy, links and prompts come in as props or `labels`.
